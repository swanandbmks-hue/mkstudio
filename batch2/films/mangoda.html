Mangoda — Marathi Drama Produced In-House at MK Studio

Drama / Love · Marathi Film · 2019

Mangoda is a rare example of end-to-end in-house filmmaking at MK Studio Pune — produced by K. Jyotiram and M.K. Dhumal under the MK Studio banner, with every stage of post-production completed within the studio. Directed by K. Jyotiram, the film blends drama and romance in a uniquely Marathi narrative. Post-production credits include editing by Chinmay Ghare and Ravi Nimbalkaar, sound design and foley/SFX by Mahesh Naik, background music by Raju Chandgude, D.I. by Ravi Nimbalkaar, and trailer by Rishikesh Chavan. A testament to MK Studio's full-service filmmaking capability.

📽️ About MK Studio Pune

MK Studio Pune is a full-service film production and post-production studio established in 2008 by Machindra Kisan Dhumal. With 14 Marathi and Hindi films to its credit, MK Studio provides complete film production, equipment rental, recording, editing, D.I. colour grading, sound design, VFX, DCP, and film marketing services — all under one roof in Pune, Maharashtra, India.
