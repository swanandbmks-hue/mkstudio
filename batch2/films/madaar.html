Madaar — Upcoming Marathi Drama by Mangesh Badar

Drama · Marathi Film · 2019

Madaar is a powerful Marathi drama co-produced and directed by Mangesh Badar, alongside producers Milind Shinde and M.K. Dhumal under M.M. Format. Shot in 2019, the film is nearing completion with MK Studio Pune providing the full post-production suite — editing and D.I. colour grading by Vishal Sarode, sound design, sound mixing, and audio re-recording by Mahesh Naik. Madaar is a story rooted in the human experience, crafted by a team deeply committed to authentic Marathi storytelling.

📽️ About MK Studio Pune

MK Studio Pune is a full-service film production and post-production studio established in 2008 by Machindra Kisan Dhumal. With 14 Marathi and Hindi films to its credit, MK Studio provides complete film production, equipment rental, recording, editing, D.I. colour grading, sound design, VFX, DCP, and film marketing services — all under one roof in Pune, Maharashtra, India.
