Atoot — Marathi Love Story by Suresh Khude

Love · Marathi Film · 2018

Atoot — meaning unbreakable — is a Marathi romance film that lives up to its name. Directed and produced by Suresh Khude, this intimate love story explores the strength of emotional bonds that defy circumstance and time. MK Studio Pune's contribution includes complete video editing by Vijaykumar Mali and professional song recording by Mahesh Naik, ensuring that every beat of the narrative and every note of the soundtrack serves the emotional core of the story.

📽️ About MK Studio Pune

MK Studio Pune is a full-service film production and post-production studio established in 2008 by Machindra Kisan Dhumal. With 14 Marathi and Hindi films to its credit, MK Studio provides complete film production, equipment rental, recording, editing, D.I. colour grading, sound design, VFX, DCP, and film marketing services — all under one roof in Pune, Maharashtra, India.
